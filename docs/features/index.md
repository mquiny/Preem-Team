<span class="pt-feature-eyebrow">Survival & Immersion</span>

### Dark Future

Night City stops being a theme park and starts being a place you have to
survive. Hunger, thirst, fatigue, and addiction all become real pressures on
V, environmental hazards actually matter, and healing items get scarce
enough that a firefight has actual stakes again. Everything is
tunable — dial it up for a proper survival run, or just enough to make combat
feel dangerous.

<span class="pt-feature-eyebrow">Camera & Exploration</span>

### Third Person

A full third-person camera for walking, driving, and combat,
so every cyberware sculpt, outfit, and paint job you've agonized over
actually gets seen, not just glimpsed in a mirror or a menu preview.
Switches back to first person instantly whenever you want it in the CET Menu.

<span class="pt-feature-caption">This mod is currently very new, so expect bugs and issues to occur while this mod matures</span>

<span class="pt-feature-eyebrow">Vehicles</span>

### New Vehicles

Several new rides added straight into world spawns and available to purchase on vs computer
from the everyday to the absurd. Two favorites from the current lineup: the
**Toyota Supra Mk3**, a proper street-tuner icon, and the **Lamborghini
Miura P400 SV**, a slice of pre-War old-world excess that has no business
looking this good parked outside a ripperdoc. Cruise around the City of Dreams In Style.

<span class="pt-feature-eyebrow">Cyberware</span>

### Sandevistan OS Showcase

Time-slowing was always cool — now it looks the part. Reworked Sandevistan
operating systems bring new activation effects, screen-space distortion, and
sound design on top of tuned slow-mo gameplay, so ripping through a room full
of gonks feels like the netrunner power fantasy it was always supposed to be.

<span class="pt-feature-eyebrow">Combat</span>

### Enhanced AI & Combat

A combat and enemy-behavior overhaul that stops NPCs from standing around
waiting to be shot. Enemies flank, take cover, call in backup, and actually
react to what V is doing — turning most firefights from a shooting gallery
into something you need an actual plan for.

<span class="pt-feature-eyebrow">Character Creator</span>

### New Character Creator Options

A huge expansion to character creation — more body options, skin and makeup
detail, tattoos, hairstyles, and cyberware variants than base-game V ever
had. The result is a lot more of *your* V and a lot less "everyone in Night
City looks the same."
